# Korea accounting, tax, and financial-planning exams

Research date: 2026-11-11. Nine Tier 1 records. 전산회계 and 전산세무 are four independent nationally accredited private qualifications of the Korean Association of Certified Tax Accountants, not stages of one credential and not the Korea Chamber of Commerce and Industry exam 전산회계운용사. Korean CFP is the Korea program, not the US CFP Board exam. FAT, TAT, IFRS관리사, and 관세사 were not researched.

Pass-rate percentages below are either the figure printed by the citing article or the examinee-to-passer ratio rounded to two decimals when both counts were printed. Rates were not back-calculated from a percentage alone.
