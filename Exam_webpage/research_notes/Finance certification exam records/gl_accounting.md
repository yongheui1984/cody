California. It is treated as the largest-population US jurisdiction. The main sit rule and the main license rule are the California Board of Accountancy rules in force on 2026-11-11, not a 150-hour sit rule. Other states were not researched and are named only as a difference the page cannot judge.

- US CPA: four stages. Three Core sections plus one chosen Discipline. BAR, ISC and TCP stay inside one stage. Their quarterly rates are stored in unknown_reasons and are not averaged.
