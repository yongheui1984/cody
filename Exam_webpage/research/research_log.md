   - *Status:* Abolished and restructured under the Capital Markets Act amendments.
   - *Resolution:* Replaced by `kr_fund_advisor` (펀드투자권유자문인력) and `kr_sec_advisor` (증권투자권유자문인력) for financial institution employees, and `kr_fund_solicitor` (펀드투자권유대행인) and `kr_sec_solicitor` (증권투자권유대행인) for non-employees.
   - *Source:* Financial Services Commission (FSC) Public Notice & KOFIA Regulations.
2. **파생상품투자상담사 (Derivatives Investment Advisor):**
   - *Status:* Abolished and replaced by `kr_deriv_advisor` (파생상품투자권유자문인력) and `kr_deriv_solicitor` (파생상품투자권유대행인).
   - *Source:* KOFIA.
3. **일반운용전문인력 / 간접투자증권운용전문인력:**
   - *Status:* Merged into `kr_cim` (투자자산운용사). Holders of the legacy titles are granted specific course exemptions in CIM.
   - *Source:* KOFIA Certification Division.
4. **신용위험분석사 (Credit Risk Analyst - CRA / KBI candidate list):**
   - *Status:* Merged / offered as `여신심사역` (Credit Reviewer - CRA) by the Korea Institute of Finance (KBI). Investigated and confirmed that KBI currently grants `여신심사역` as the premier credit analysis title beyond CCA.
   - *Resolution:* Documented under `kr_cra`.

## 3. Candidate Additions Beyond Base List

To maximize utility for university students and job seekers targeting the Korean financial sector, the following high-impact items were proactively researched:

1. **`kr_bok_g5` (Bank of Korea G5 Recruitment Exam):**
   - *Reason:* The benchmark public recruitment examination for Korean macro-financial economics. University students frequently compare academic credentials (CFA/KICPA) against BOK preparation load.
2. **`kr_fss_exam` (Financial Supervisory Service Recruitment Exam):**
   - *Reason:* The regulatory authority governing all financial institutions. Represents the pinnacle of financial regulation, law, and accounting recruitment.
3. **`kr_bank_ncs` (Major Commercial Banks NCS & Finance Knowledge Exam):**
   - *Reason:* The primary pathway for banking employment (KB, Shinhan, Hana, Woori, NH). Serves as the real-world milestone against which certificate holders leverage their qualifications.
4. **`us_ea` (IRS Enrolled Agent):**
   - *Reason:* Rapidly growing in popularity among Korean accounting students and tax professionals due to US-Korea cross-border tax advisory demand and accessible eligibility (no degree required).
5. **`kr_customs_broker` (관세사):**
   - *Reason:* Recognized as one of Korea's Top 8 Professional Licenses (8대 전문직), intimately linked with foreign exchange regulation, trade financing, and customs valuation.

The following regional Tier 2 items mentioned in the candidate ideation list were deferred:

- **India NISM / AMFI Certifications & ICAI CA:** Excluded as Korean employment relevance is near-zero; strictly regional to the Indian domestic regulatory framework.
- **China Fund / Securities Practitioner & CICPA:** Excluded due to strict Chinese domestic residency/employment requirements and exam administration conducted exclusively in simplified Chinese within mainland China.
- **Japan JSDA / FP / Nissho Boki:** Japanese domestic licenses (FP技能士, 日商簿記) are localized to Japanese GAAP/tax laws, relevant primarily for candidates relocating to Tokyo financial markets.
- **US Insurance ChFC / CLU:** American College designations with limited recognition in Korea compared to CFP and CFA.
